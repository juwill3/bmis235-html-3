🥇 Diana Taurasi - The WNBA Scoring Queen 🏀

Diana Taurasi is one of the greatest WNBA players of all time. She played for the Phoenix Mercury from 2004 to 2024 and became the WNBA's all-time leading scorer.

She won 3 WNBA championships, 6 Olympic gold medals, and the 2009 WNBA MVP award. She is known for her amazing shooting, confidence, and competitive attitude.
